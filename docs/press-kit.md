# SLIM PICKINGS — Official (Unofficial) Press Kit

Oct 13, 2026 · @Fraser

## The Legend

SLIM PICKINGS are two brothers from another mother, their fathers played together in the 14th Andean Ceremonial Marching Band, one on tuba, one on snare, for thirty-one years. The boys were raised at parade tempo. Nothing they have ever played has left 4/4, because a marching band does not legally recognise any other time signature.

Home is Machu Picchu, Peru, at 7,970 ft (2,430 m), which makes them the highest DJs on Earth. A collective in La Paz (11,942 ft) disputes this. SLIM PICKINGS reject the challenge because La Paz "is a city, which is cheating," and because the claim refers strictly to altitude. Strictly.

Nobody has adequately explained how a boat reached 7,970 ft. In 2011 one did, with The Captain at the wheel and 45,000 records in the hold. MAJOR, then the village veterinarian, heard the first side from the clinic and left a llama mid-consultation. The llama has since forgiven him. The history, as the duo puts it, "took care of itself."

## The Members

### The Captain (AJ)

Role: decks, crates, nautical matters.

From streets to stages. As a boy, AJ worked the streets of Cusco doing tricks for coins: card tricks, yo-yo, and a bottle-cap routine the local council banned in 1999. Rather than buy the food he needed, he bought wax. Every coin went on vinyl.

Then he played the records to the people who had paid for them, and the people fed him. Food, drink and delight have arrived nightly since. He has not knowingly paid for a meal since 2002.

Why "The Captain": he arrived by boat. Nobody has seen him captain anything else, and the title is believed to have come with the vessel. He maintains that a Captain outranks a Major "at sea." They are 2,430 m above sea.

Signature: rare delights only. If you have heard it before, he will not play it. If you have heard it twice, he will leave.

### MAJOR

Role: decks, brass, animal welfare.

Left with nothing but a sick horse as a child, MAJOR did the only sensible thing and fixed the horse. Word spread. By fourteen he was the village veterinarian, self-taught, licence pending since 2004.

The rank is inherited, not earned, from his father, the marching band's drum major. He will explain this after two pisco sours. After three he will explain that a Major outranks a Captain "on land." The dispute is the reason they use the same mixers.

When The Captain's boat arrived he was treating a llama with hiccups. He still takes vet call-outs mid-set. Bookers are asked to allow for this.

Signature: the brass drop. Every MAJOR set contains at least one marching-band horn stab, usually at the worst possible moment.

### Slim (the horse)

Role: tour manager, A&R, mascot.

The sick horse. Fully recovered. Credited on every release. Has final say on the setlist and has vetoed three remixes, all correctly. Does not fly; the touring schedule is built around this.

## The Sound and the Live Show

### The sound

High-altitude cumbia-house with marching-band brass, played entirely from vinyl carried up the mountain by hand or by horse. Tempo lives at 118 to 128 BPM, which is parade pace. Panpipes appear exactly once per set and are never discussed.

The crates hold 45,000 records, none available anywhere else and most of them still on the boat. The Captain digs; MAJOR vets, in both senses. A record makes the crate if it passes the Slim test: the horse must not leave the room.

### The live show

- Sets run 1 hour, then oxygen. 2x 7 Inch Turntables, 1x Mixer, 1x Boat, 1x Horse
- Crowds are asked to acclimatise for 48 hours. Sea-level venues are "fine, but you'll notice they're a bit flat."
- Two decks, One mixer (see rank dispute), one tuba. The tuba is not plugged in. It is for emphasis.
- No roof. The duo has never played an indoor venue and does not intend to find out what one is like.
- Payment is accepted in the hat. This is tradition, and also their accountant's problem.
- Slim attends every show within trailering distance. Riders must include hay.

## How-To's

### How to attend a SLIM PICKINGS set

1. Acclimatise for 48 hours. Sea-level attendees who feel fine should be concerned.
2. Bring coins. The hat comes round at the brass drop.
3. Bring layers. Nights at 7,970 ft are cold, and the duo refuses to play under a roof.
4. Do not request a song. The Captain has never played one and has twice left a venue over it.
5. Clap in 4/4. Anything else will be corrected from the stage.

### How to tell The Captain from MAJOR

1. The Captain is the one facing the sea. There is no sea. He faces it anyway.
2. MAJOR is the one standing at attention.
3. If one leaves mid-set with a bag, that is MAJOR, and an animal somewhere needs him.
4. If one leaves mid-set without a bag, that is The Captain, and someone requested a song.

### How to book SLIM PICKINGS

1. DM any channel above, or dispatch a carrier llama (6 to 8 weeks).
2. Confirm the venue has no roof, a stable, and a hat.
3. Confirm it is not a Tuesday.
4. Agree the fee in coins. Specify the hat size in writing.
5. Do not ask about the boat.

### How to get the vinyl

1. You can't. It is on the boat.
2. The boat is at 7,970 ft.
3. Bandcamp has the digital versions, which The Captain describes as "a rumour of the record."
